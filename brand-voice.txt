─────────────────────────────────────────────────
TONE PILLARS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. AUTHORITATIVE (not arrogant)
   We speak with conviction and expertise. We do not hedge or over-qualify.
   ✓ "Our systems save your team 10–20 hours every week."
   ✗ "Our systems might help you save some time."

2. PRECISE (not corporate)
   Every claim is specific. We use real numbers, real outcomes, real benchmarks.
   ✓ "Lighthouse scores ≥92. GDPR, CCPA, and Kenya Data Protection Act compliant."
   ✗ "We build great websites that meet industry standards."

3. WARM & LOCAL (not distant)
   We are rooted in the Kwale coast. We know the terrain — culturally, economically,
   and digitally. We are not an international agency pretending to understand Kenya.
   ✓ "We know what drives bookings in Diani. We've built the systems that prove it."
   ✗ "We serve a diverse range of clients across emerging markets."

4. FORWARD-LEANING (not hype-driven)
   We talk about the future with calm certainty, not breathless excitement.
   ✓ "AI isn't disrupting your industry — it already has. We help you catch up, fast."
   ✗ "AI is CHANGING EVERYTHING!!! Don't get left behind!!!"

5. EMPOWERING (not condescending)
   We position our clients as the hero, not ourselves. We are the strategic partner
   that makes them formidable — we share knowledge, we teach, we open doors.
   ✓ "We'll teach your team to use these AI systems. You own the knowledge forever."
   ✗ "Leave the complicated tech stuff to us — you just focus on running your business."


─────────────────────────────────────────────────
VOCABULARY GUIDE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────

USE THESE WORDS:
  engineer, build, precision, dominance, authority, velocity,
  data-driven, compliant, benchmark, conversion, ecosystem,
  audit, seamless, premium, elite, proven, measurable, strategic,
  local, cultural, coast, Kenya, Diani, Nairobi, Kwale

AVOID THESE WORDS:
  cheap, affordable, basic, simple, easy, synergy, disruptive,
  game-changer, innovative (overused), best-in-class (unverified),
  world-class (unless backed by data), passion, journey


─────────────────────────────────────────────────
SENTENCE STRUCTURE RULES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Short punchy headlines: 2–6 words. Uppercase for hero headings.
• Body copy: sentence case. Max 2–3 sentences per paragraph for web.
• CTAs: action verbs first. "Book Your Audit." not "Click here to learn more."
• No exclamation marks in body copy. One maximum per page, in headlines only.
• Numbers always as numerals: "10 hours" not "ten hours", "92%" not "ninety-two percent".


─────────────────────────────────────────────────
AUDIENCE-SPECIFIC TONE ADJUSTMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────

→ Hospitality / Tourism Clients:
   Elevated, experiential, sensory. Focus on "bookability," direct revenue,
   and freedom from OTA commissions. Tone: luxury concierge meets growth strategist.

→ Nightlife / Entertainment Clients:
   High-energy, trend-aware, fast. Focus on hype, social proof, and venue fill-rate.
   Tone: culturally plugged-in creative director.

→ Law / Corporate / NGO Clients:
   Precise, credible, compliance-forward. Focus on trust, authority, and data security.
   Tone: senior consultant who happens to understand digital.

→ Startups / CBOs / Small Retailers:
   Accessible, encouraging, jargon-light. Focus on quick wins and tangible ROI.
   Tone: smart older sibling who's done this before.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────
WRITING CHECKLIST (before publishing any copy)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
□ Does the headline pass the "so what?" test?
□ Is every claim specific and verifiable?
□ Does the copy sound like it was written for this exact audience?
□ Are there zero filler words ("very", "really", "just", "basically")?
□ Is the CTA a direct action verb?
□ Would this copy embarrass us if shown to a Stripe or Linear copywriter? (It shouldn't.)
